Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy? +
We offer a 30-day return policy for all products in original condition. Electronics must be unopened and accessories should be in like-new condition. Contact our support team to initiate a return.
Do you offer international shipping? +
Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. You can calculate shipping costs during checkout.
How long does shipping take? +
Standard shipping within the US takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available for an additional fee. International shipping typically takes 7-14 business days.
Do you price match? +
Yes, we offer price matching on identical products from authorized retailers. Contact our customer service within 7 days of purchase with the competitor's current price, and we'll match it.
What warranty do you offer? +
Most products come with a 1-year manufacturer's warranty. Extended warranty options are available for purchase. Check individual product pages for specific warranty information.
